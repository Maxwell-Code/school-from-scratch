
With larger schools the main method of payment is usually a third party app separated from banking services. To make money these apps charge a fee (usually around 0.7% of the total money sent). As a brand new and relatively unknown school The School From Scratch does not get consistent funding and these fees, even ones as small as 0.7%, add up. To avoid them we decided to use Zelle as our primary payment method. If you cannot complete your payment through Zelle you please contact us and we'll look at other methods.

1. Open your bank's app and choose Send with Zelle.
2. Send to the Zelle tag **{ZELLE_RECIPIENT}**. Before you send, check that the name shown is **{ZELLE_RECIPIENT_NAME}**.
3. Enter the amount.
4. If you are making a tuition payment in the memo, write your child's name and what the payment is for. 
#### Thank you!
