This is also a space for parent learning and for parents to partner with teachers in close observation, documentation, and inquiry.

The ask from parents is:

1. parents commit to attending a monthly meeting for 2 hrs exploring with other parents and teachers – matters of learning, education, and life that are not focused on their own child (as there will be separate forums/conversation for this)
2. parents are invited to join a weekend camping trip with children two-three times in a year
3. parents maintain a notebook to document observations, activities, and questions of their child (this is an important resource for teachers and also contributes to creating a consonance between life at school and life at home).
4. one day of the week is likely to include visits to the house of one of the students (by rotation). This is intended to get an understanding of the home environment, support the parent & family, and develop a sense of community or shared engagement in each other’s lives.

Parent-school or parent-teacher relationships are not customer-service provider oriented or merely a childcare focus but rather one of *mutual learning*. This requires sustained commitment and engagement from all of us.
